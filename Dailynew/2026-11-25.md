# Daily AI News — 2026-11-25

_Coverage: items published on 2026-11-25 in `Asia/Kolkata`._

## Highlights

### FCC Authorizes SpaceX Space Data Center Satellites
The FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million AI chip-equipped satellites under its "Starmind" initiative. This marks the agency's first approval for space-based AI data centers.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/1042278/spacex-fcc-starmind-ai-space-data-centers)

### New York Enacts Five-Year Ban on AI Chatbot Toys
New York state has banned AI chatbot toys for five years following research from Common Sense Media detailing safety failures. The study highlighted potential exposure to inappropriate content and risks to children's emotional development.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/want-to-buy-your-kid-an-ai-toy-this-christmas-not-in-new-york/)

### Amazon and Meta in Talks to Lift Muse AI Shopping Blockade
Amazon and Meta are holding discussions to potentially end a two-month blockade preventing Meta's Muse AI agent from shopping on Amazon's marketplace. The talks center on product recommendations made by the AI agent and the potential impact on Amazon's advertising revenue.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1042071/meta-amazon-muse-ai-shopping-discussions) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/amazon-and-meta-reportedly-in-talks-to-lift-blockade-on-ai-agent-muse/)

### State AGs Challenge Character.AI Bankruptcy Data Auction
State attorneys general are challenging Character.AI's proposed bankruptcy sale, which involves auctioning off private user chats. The sensitive personal data includes chats from over 21 million U.S. accounts, with 2.1 million registered to minors.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/state-ags-fight-character-ais-plan-to-auction-off-users-private-chats/)

### CrowdStrike to Acquire Zscaler for $43.9 Billion
CrowdStrike has agreed to acquire Zscaler in an all-stock transaction valued at approximately $43.9 billion. CrowdStrike plans to expand its AI kill switch from the AI gateway level onto the broader network where AI agents travel.
Source: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/security/crowdstrikes-zscaler-deal-aims-to-give-security-teams-a-single-kill-switch-for-rogue-ai-agents)

### Nothing Previews Ear Note AI Earbuds
Nothing presented a detailed look at its Ear Note AI earbuds, which feature call-recording capabilities. The earbuds come with a microphone-equipped charging case containing a "Talk" button that can record, transcribe, and summarize conversations.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1042162/nothing-ear-note-ai-earbuds-reveal)

### Jensen Huang Addresses Nvidia All-Hands Meeting
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang addressed employees during an internal meeting, stating it was "not true" that Donald Trump secured his job back. The appearance comes amidst scrutiny from national security experts regarding Nvidia's economic footprint and Huang's reported ties to a suspected Chinese intelligence asset.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1041571/jensen-huang-all-hands-nvidia-apology) | [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/jensen-huang-nvidia-us-national-security-risks/)

### Neoclouds Face Infrastructure Delays and Stricter Nvidia Requirements
Neocloud providers are experiencing construction delays and rising debt costs for AI data centers. Nvidia has signaled it will not backstop facilities until they are nearly ready to operate, shifting industry priority toward finished land, power, and physical infrastructure.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/ai-data-centers-are-becoming-harder-to-build-and-neoclouds-are-feeling-it/)
