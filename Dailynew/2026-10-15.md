### Meta releases Muse Spark Open AI model
Meta has released Muse Spark Open, an open-weight multimodal mixture-of-experts AI model. Meta claims it is the first model in its class capable of running on a desktop using two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios with the latest MLX framework.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/news/1012293/meta-apple-muse-spark-open-ai-model-mlx)

### Google launches AI Universal Cart tool in the US
Google has officially launched its Universal Cart AI shopping tool in the United States across Google Search and the Gemini app. Originally previewed at Google I/O in May, the tool is designed to simplify online shopping ahead of the holiday season.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/googles-universal-cart-shopping-tool-launches-in-the-us/)

### Microsoft Research introduces Magnetar for proof-based computing
Microsoft Research detailed Magnetar, a framework designed to bring zero-knowledge proofs into mainstream general-purpose computing. The tool supports standard programming languages and quantum-resistant security, allowing computing results to be verified without exposing sensitive data or re-running workloads.
Source: [Microsoft Research Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/proof-based-general-purpose-computing-with-magnetar/)
