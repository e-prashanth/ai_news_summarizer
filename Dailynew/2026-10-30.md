## Highlights

### Google Satellite Transmits First AI Response from Orbit
Google has booted up on-board Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) on its first AI data center satellite. Running Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 model, the satellite generated a haiku about space and successfully transmitted the response back to Earth.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1023528/google-project-suncatcher-ai-satellite-transmission)

### OpenAI to Support Shared Robots.txt Signals for Training Preferences

Source: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/helping-websites-express-ai-training-preferences)

### Pope Leo XIV Calls for Disarmament of Autonomous Weapons
Pope Leo XIV urged United Nations member states to take concrete steps toward banning lethal autonomous weapons, stating that machines are tools rather than persons. He emphasized that humans must retain the responsibility to direct, modify, or reject AI applications.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1023188/pope-leo-disarm-ai)

### Wikimedia Foundation Weighs In on AI Copyright Litigation
The Wikimedia Foundation has taken a legal position in landmark AI copyright cases, urging courts to protect large language model training under fair use. However, the organization argued that fair use protection should not extend to chatbots that directly regurgitate copyrighted works to users.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1022880/wikipedia-openai-new-york-times-copyright)

### Personal AI Agent Accessed Secure Pentagon Network
