### OpenAI Announces Grid Protectors Program for Data Centers
OpenAI introduced its Grid Protectors Program, an initiative designed to work with utilities to maintain power grid reliability and help protect consumer electricity costs. The program will pilot at an upcoming data center in Effingham County, Georgia, with promises to free up to 1,000 megawatts of power during extreme weather or grid emergencies.
Source: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/grid-protectors-program) | [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1024604/openai-data-center-grid-protection-program)
