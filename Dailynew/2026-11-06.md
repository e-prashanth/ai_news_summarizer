## Highlights

### OpenAI Introduces BioBasicsBench for Wet-Lab Science
OpenAI has launched BioBasicsBench, a benchmark containing 100 tasks designed to evaluate how AI agents handle everyday wet-lab science. The benchmark measures an agent's ability to interpret raw experimental data and execute research workflows.
Source: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/measuring-how-ai-agents-handle-wet-lab-science)

### OpenAI Partners with Hacktron to Bolster System Security
OpenAI is partnering with cybersecurity firm Hacktron to discover system vulnerabilities and test connections with third-party services. The collaboration aims to use frontier AI to strengthen OpenAI's platform defenses.
Source: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-hacktron)

### Voters Approve New Restrictions on Data Center Development
Following the U.S. midterm elections, voters passed measures imposing tighter restrictions on data center development in 40 out of 41 local ballot initiatives across the country. The broad victories for Democratic candidates and local ballot measures signal growing public pushback against data center expansion.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/podcast/how-a-blue-wave-midterm-might-reshape-the-ai-industry/)

### Amazon Launches Text-Based AI Shopping for Alexa
Amazon introduced a feature allowing users to message the Alexa for Shopping assistant to find and buy items. Users can send text descriptions or photos of products to receive recommendations and complete purchases directly via text.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1028587/amazon-alexa-shopping-ai-text-orders)

### Ramp Data Shows AI Enterprise Spending Stalling
Business expense data from Ramp indicates that enterprise AI spending has flattened despite continuous growth in AI usage. Price reductions and stricter internal cost controls by major enterprise spenders have contributed to the spending plateau.
