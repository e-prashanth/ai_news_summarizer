## Highlights

### Google Adds Option to Remove Visible AI Watermarks
Google is allowing users to turn off visible watermarks on AI-generated images, videos, and music via a new "Media watermark" toggle in Gemini and its video generator, Flow. Toggling the setting off removes the visible "sparkle" watermark in the bottom-right corner of generated content. Disabling this setting does not affect the invisible benchmarks used to identify AI-generated files.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/980416/google-gemini-ai-watermarks-removal) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/google-will-now-allow-users-to-remove-visible-watermark-from-its-ai-generations/)

### Apple Reportedly Trained China-Specific AI Model with Alibaba
Apple has developed a custom AI model tailored for the Chinese market with assistance from domestic tech giant Alibaba. According to a Reuters report citing three unnamed sources, the China-focused large language model was trained with Alibaba's direct support.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/980160/apple-intelligence-china-custom-ai-model-alibaba)

### Meta Releases Open-Weight Model Glimmer
Meta has released Glimmer, an open-weight AI model that users can download and run on their own hardware. The release contrasts with Meta's more powerful model, Muse Spark, which remains restricted behind company APIs, and was accompanied by a letter from Mark Zuckerberg arguing that AI should be broadly accessible rather than controlled by a few labs.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/video/does-mark-zuckerberg-really-believe-ai-is-for-everyone/)
