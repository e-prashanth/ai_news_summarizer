## Highlights

### Security researchers use Claude to breach OpenAI accounts
A team of three independent security researchers at Hacktron used Anthropic's Claude Opus 4.8 and 5 to access OpenAI employee accounts in under 72 hours. The breach allowed researchers to gain access to OpenAI's GitHub repository, "Monorepo," which reportedly holds the company's algorithmic secrets.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/997444/openai-hack-claude-heif-heist)

### Anthropic CEO proposes plan to "Pace the Frontier" of AI
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei outlined a proposal to pace frontier AI development following an AI doomsday warning from an Anthropic researcher. The initiative relies on independent safety evaluators and coordination between AI labs in democratic countries, receiving support within the industry as well as pushback from Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/video/dario-amodei-and-other-ai-leaders-want-to-pace-the-frontier-buthow/)

### Google refocuses CC AI agent on household coordination
Google is refocusing its CC AI agent to help families manage household tasks, schedules, and emails. The agent can handle shared calendars, fill out forms, generate shopping lists, and plan meals.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/googles-new-cc-is-an-ai-agent-that-helps-families-run-their-households/)

### Disney hires former Character.AI CEO as first CTO
Disney appointed the former CEO of Character.AI as its first-ever chief technology officer. Disney had previously sent a cease-and-desist letter to Character.AI accusing the startup of copying Disney characters.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/disneys-first-cto-led-an-ai-startup-it-once-accused-of-copying-its-characters/)

### Anthropic relaunches Projects in Claude Code for multi-agent workflows
Anthropic updated the Projects feature in Claude Code to manage multiple AI agents running parallel task threads in the cloud. The platform coordinates these agents using shared memory, common goals, and a shared library of files and artifacts.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/997134/anthropic-claude-code-projects)

### Hollywood labor groups urge focus on current AI usage
Entertainment labor groups are encouraging the public to prioritize immediate AI impacts over tech industry warnings regarding existential threats. Major studios and film startups continue to integrate generative AI tools into their operations.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/997358/what-hollywood-thinks-about-existential-ai-warnings)

### Meta responds to Polish report on scam advertisements
Meta issued a statement rejecting a report by the Instrat foundation regarding scam ads in Poland, calling the findings misleading and noting the report was authored by a law firm representing an individual suing Meta. Meta stated it continues to combat fraudulent advertising using advanced AI systems, user protection mechanisms, and industry collaboration.
Source: [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/prostujemy-fakty-o-walce-z-oszukanczymi-reklamami-w-polsce/)
