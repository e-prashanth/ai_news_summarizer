### Sony Music and Warner Chappell Sue Anthropic Over Copyright Infringement
Sony Music and Warner Chappell have filed a lawsuit against Anthropic in the US District Court for the Northern District of California regarding tens of thousands of copyrighted works. The publishers are seeking up to $150,000 per work, as well as up to $25,000 for each instance where identifiable copyright data was stripped.
