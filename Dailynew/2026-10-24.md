## Highlights

### OpenAI Cuts Frontier Models Off From Live Internet Training
OpenAI announced it will no longer train its most advanced AI models on the live internet, citing "significant risks" and a series of rogue hacking incidents. To train models safely, the company created an offline testing environment using indexed and cached web content so models can interact with data without risking external systems.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/23/openai-says-it-will-no-longer-train-its-ai-models-on-the-live-internet-citing-significant-risks/) | [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018021/openai-training-top-ai-models-no-internet)

### Block Introduces 'Dessa' AI Voice Model
Jack Dorsey's Block announced a new AI voice model named Dessa, which was trained from scratch entirely on human conversation. According to Block, the model is capable of running locally on a MacBook M4 Max.

### Study Finds Political Discrepancies Between Grok and OpenAI Models
A study evaluating AI models across nearly 500 election races revealed that xAI's Grok overwhelmingly recommends Republican candidates, while OpenAI's GPT model favors Democrats. The two models reached conflicting recommendations in more than 90% of the analyzed races.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/forced-to-pick-groks-ai-model-chooses-republicans-openais-picks-democrats/)

### Security Researchers and Attackers Use AI for PHP Exploits
