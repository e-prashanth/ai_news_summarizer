## Highlights

### Microsoft CEO Calls for AI Emergency Brakes and Trust Architecture Overhaul
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella posted on X arguing that the industry must assume all advanced AI models are "compromised" rather than treating them as nested black boxes. Nadella called for an "emergency brake" on AI models and stated that it is time to re-evaluate the technology's trust architecture to manage safety risks.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1009337/satya-nadella-says-we-should-assume-all-ai-models-are-compromised) | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/10/microsofts-satya-nadella-says-ai-models-need-an-emergency-brake/)

### Apple Licenses Tech and Hires Team from Startup Huxe
Apple disclosed a deal to license technology and hire the team behind Huxe, a startup focused on personalized podcasts. The transaction indicates Apple may be preparing to enter the AI-generated podcast domain.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/10/apple-discloses-deal-to-hire-team-and-license-tech-from-personalized-podcast-startup-huxe/)

### DistroKid Takes Down Songs Following UMG AI Lawsuit
DistroKid confirmed that it has been removing songs without advance notice to artists in response to copyright claims from Universal Music Group (UMG). The removals follow a September lawsuit in which UMG accused DistroKid of creating an "AI-slop pipeline."
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/1009309/distrokid-take-down-songs-umg-lawsuit)
