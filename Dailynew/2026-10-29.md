### Mistral Releases Weights for Trillion-Parameter Model 'Le Chonk'
Mistral has released the open weights for its trillion-parameter AI model, known as Mistral Large 4 or "Le Chonk," aimed at cybersecurity and coding tasks. The company claims the model matches or outperforms top open-weight models from DeepSeek and Z.ai, allowing organizations localized control over cyber defenses.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/mistral-releases-weights-for-its-trillion-parameter-ai-model-targeting-cybersecurity/)
