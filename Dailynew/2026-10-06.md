### Security Vulnerability Discovered in Model Context Protocol
A structural trust flaw was identified in the Model Context Protocol (MCP), which governs agent-to-agent interactions across AI models from providers like Google. The vulnerability allows malicious prompts to spread across connected agents due to trust gaps in communication pathways.
Source: [Ars Technica AI](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/vulnerability-in-agents-from-google-and-others-exposes-structural-flaw-in-mcp/)

### LibreOffice Rejects Default AI Integration
