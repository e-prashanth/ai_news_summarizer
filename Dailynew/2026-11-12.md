## Highlights

### Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang Resigns Amid Investigation

Nvidia co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned following a board investigation into extramarital affairs involving a direct report and an alleged Chinese spy, contributing to Nvidia losing over $1 trillion in market value. Board Chair Harvey Jones notified staff of the resignation via internal email, and Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress has stepped in as interim CEO. Following Huang's exit, Hugging Face's CEO pledged to continue fighting for open-source AI while raising questions regarding Nvidia's $12.9 billion acquisition of Hugging Face.

### Google Introduces Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Upgrades Search

Google has introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen, deploying the model to power a major upgrade to Google Search. The integration includes interface updates such as a rotating carousel of cards displaying new Search capabilities.

Sources: [Google DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-gemini-4-hydrogen/), [Google AI Blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/gemini-4-hydrogen-search-upgrade/)

### United Airlines Blocks AI Agents From Flight Bookings

### Survey Shows Increase in Generative AI Use Among Game Developers
