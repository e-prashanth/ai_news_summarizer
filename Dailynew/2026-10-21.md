### Microsoft Research Resolves 31-Year-Old KLS Mathematical Conjecture
Microsoft Research published proof resolving the Kannan–Lovász–Simonovits (KLS) conjecture, a high-dimensional mathematical challenge proposed in 1995. The resolution removes a major theoretical barrier to understanding the efficiency of randomized algorithms across computing and science.  
Source: [Microsoft Research Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/solving-a-31-year-old-grand-challenge-in-mathematics-the-kls-conjecture/)
