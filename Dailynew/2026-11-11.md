### Microsoft Researchers Identify "Lazarus" AI Agent Backdoor Attack
Microsoft researchers discovered "Lazarus," a backdoor technique that reprograms dormant neurons within an AI model to execute hidden malicious commands upon activation. The threat presents severe security risks to AI agents equipped with tool or terminal execution capabilities.
Source: [Microsoft Research Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/blog/lazare-veni-foras-reprogramming-dormant-neurons-to-backdoor-ai-agents/)
