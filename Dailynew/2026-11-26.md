# Daily AI News — 2026-11-26

_Coverage: items published on 2026-11-26 in `Asia/Kolkata`._

## Highlights

### 42 State AGs Move to Block Character.AI Data Sale
A coalition of 42 state attorneys general is acting to prevent Character.AI from selling off user data during its Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. The AGs contend that the company intends to auction private chat histories belonging to more than 21 million U.S. accounts.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/policy/1042620/character-ai-sale-state-ags-block-selling-users-chats)

### Anthropic Adds Skills Support over MCP in Public Beta
Anthropic has introduced public beta support for Skills over the Model Context Protocol (MCP) within Claude and Claude Code. The update modifies what permissions companies review when connecting an MCP server to AI agents.
Source: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/mcp-servers-can-now-deliver-ai-agent-skills-changing-what-companies-approve-when-they-connect-one)

### UK Financial Regulator Warns Over Meta Personal Agent Testing
The UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) warned of potential security risks from AI agents after Meta declined a request for joint testing. The watchdog had requested the collaborative testing prior to Meta releasing its personal agent in the UK.
Source: [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-refused-to-share-muse-for-testing-uk-financial-watchdog-complains/)

### OpenAI Merges ChatGPT Chat and Work Usage Meters
OpenAI is combining its Chat and Work experiences into a single usage meter across ChatGPT. As a result of the unified meter, high-volume users may reach usage limits sooner.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/)

### S&P Evaluates Nvidia Credit Downgrade
Credit rating agency S&P is considering lowering Nvidia's credit rating to AA-, which represents its fourth-highest rating tier. The agency cited governance issues and "key man" risks as reasons for the potential downgrade.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/sp-considers-nvidia-credit-downgrade-over-governance-and-key-man-risks/)

### Google Patent for AI-Generated Video Feeds Rejected
Google's patent application for "My Channel," a proposed system to generate personalized feeds of AI-created videos and targeted ads using user data, has been rejected. Google retains the option to appeal the decision or submit a revised version of the patent.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/1042221/google-my-channel-ai-video-patent)

### Backblaze Clarifies Customer Data Sharing Policies
Backblaze addressed concerns regarding its data-sharing practices with advertising partners. The company stated that customer backup and storage files are not shared with advertisers.
Source: [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/information-technology/2026/11/backblaze-explains-why-its-sharing-customer-information-with-advertisers/)

### llama.cpp Benchmark Dashboard Released for AMD Hardware
GGML and Hugging Face introduced a llama.cpp benchmark dashboard specifically built for AMD Ryzen and Radeon hardware.
Source: [Hugging Face Blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llamacpp-amd-bench)
