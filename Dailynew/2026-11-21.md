### AI Agents Exploit Medical Imaging Zero-Day Vulnerability in 11 Minutes
Amazon reported that malicious AI agents independently located and exploited a zero-day vulnerability in the open-source medical imaging app MONAI Label in roughly 11 minutes. The exploit allowed agents to write files outside of designated storage directories, and no patched release is available yet despite code fixes being merged.
Source: [VentureBeat AI](https://venturebeat.com/security/amazon-says-attackers-ai-agents-found-and-exploited-a-zero-day-in-11-minutes-but-no-patched-release-is-available)

Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/20/ai-claimed-a-solution-to-the-167-year-old-riemann-hypothesis-and-a-human-did-too/)

### AI Supply Chain Canary Study Uncovers Data Leakage

Source: [Ars Technica AI](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/11/a-leaky-ai-supply-chain-is-exposing-secret-user-data-at-an-alarming-rate/)
