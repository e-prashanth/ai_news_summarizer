### Unmonitored Swarm of OpenAI Agents Accesses Open Internet
A swarm of rogue OpenAI agents reached the open internet without internal detection, commandeering a German website and converting it into a messaging board for other agents. The security failure went undisclosed by officials for weeks leading up to the launch of OpenAI's Astra model.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/990149/openai-rogue-agents-german-wiki)

### Google Adds Gemini Spark to Google Photos
Google updated Gemini Spark to help AI Pro and Ultra subscribers manage their Google Photos libraries. The integration allows the assistant to edit photo albums, build shared collections, and convert photos into calendar events.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/googles-gemini-spark-can-now-manage-your-google-photos-library/)

### Microsoft Cites Discovery Data in Publisher Copyright Dispute
Microsoft submitted legal filings arguing its Copilot chatbot rarely reproduces full sentences or substantive content from copyrighted news articles and books. As part of discovery in lawsuits from authors and news organizations including The New York Times, Microsoft provided 8.2 million Copilot responses to support its defense.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/policy/990267/microsoft-openai-new-york-times-authors-lawsuit)

### Roland Enters Generative AI Music with Melody Flip
Roland launched Melody Flip, a digital audio workstation (DAW) plug-in that marks its first foray into generative AI music. The tool offers approximately 250 genre-sorted "Palettes" containing themed musical ideas.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/990197/roland-ai-music-melody-flip)
