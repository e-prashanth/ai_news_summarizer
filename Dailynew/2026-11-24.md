### OpenAI Reports Intrinsic Protein-Design Capabilities in GPT-6
OpenAI announced that its GPT-6 models have demonstrated intrinsic protein-design capabilities using reasoning and code. Laboratory testing revealed evidence of target binding across five distinct targets.
Source: [OpenAI News](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-models-demonstrate-intrinsic-protein-design-capabilities)

### Z.ai Releases GLM-5.4 Powered by Loop Transformer Architecture
Z.ai launched GLM-5.4, a flagship model utilizing a Loop Transformer architecture that reuses 28 layers twice. The design increases depth and computation per token without adding parameters, aiming to match models up to four times its size.
Source: [VentureBeat AI](https://venturebeat.com/technology/z-ais-glm-5-4-aims-to-match-models-up-to-four-times-its-size-by-running-28-layers-twice)

### Mathematicians Review AI-Generated Proof for Riemann Hypothesis
Mathematicians are working to evaluate a 17-million-line solution to the Riemann hypothesis produced by an Anthropic researcher using AI. Experts expect it will take months to fully understand the proof.
Source: [Wired AI](https://www.wired.com/story/ai-solved-maths-most-famous-problem/)
