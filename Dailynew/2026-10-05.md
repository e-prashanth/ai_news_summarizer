## Highlights

### OpenAI Introduces Text Watermarking in the EU
OpenAI is rolling out "textGrain," an invisible, machine-readable text watermarking feature for ChatGPT and Codex, initially for users in the European Union to comply with EU provenance rules. Access to the detection technology will start with researchers. OpenAI stated that textGrain matched or exceeded other text watermarking methods like Google DeepMind's SynthID.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004880/openai-chatgpt-text-watermarks-eu-ai-act)
Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance)

### OpenAI Unveils Visual Ads Alongside ChatGPT Image Results
OpenAI announced a new visual ad format in ChatGPT that displays sponsored products and services alongside image generation results. The update also expands measurement tools, attribution partnerships, and brand suitability controls for advertisers.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/openai-launches-visual-ads-that-appear-alongside-image-generation-results/)
Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement)

### TikTok Rolls Out Conversational AI Shopping Assistant

### Sam Altman Discusses AI Tradeoffs as PR Intercepts Suicide Question
OpenAI CEO Sam Altman stated that society should tolerate negative costs like hacks and scams because the benefits created by AI will be orders of magnitude greater. Separately, an OpenAI publicist interrupted a Vanity Fair interview when editor Mark Guiducci asked Altman about a ChatGPT user's suicide, attempting to change the topic by citing time limits.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004811/openai-altman-bad-things-ai-tradeoff)
