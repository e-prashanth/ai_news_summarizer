## Highlights

### Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO
Nvidia's board of directors has reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after his resignation, determining that his return poses no national security risk following input from U.S. government officials. Donald Trump stated that he called into the Nvidia board meeting to advocate for Huang's return. 
Source: [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/nvidia-brings-back-jensen-huang-as-ceo-just-six-days-after-ruling-out-his-return)

### Amazon Acquires Decart's AI Tech for Reported $7.5 Billion
Amazon is acquiring Decart’s AI optimization technology and research team in a deal reported at $7.5 billion. The acquisition comes as Amazon prepares to sell its Trainium AI chips.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/amazon-snaps-up-decarts-ai-optimization-tech-for-7-5b-as-it-prepares-to-sell-trainium-chips/)

### Anthropic Launches Claude Ship to Review and Fix Pull Requests
Anthropic announced a new product called Claude Ship, enabling Claude AI to review, fix, and approve code pull requests autonomously. The release builds on the model's existing Code Review capabilities to help software engineers ship code.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1040134/anthropic-claude-ship-ai-pull-requests)

### Oregon and Virginia Sue SpaceX Over Grok AI Safeguards
The Attorneys General of Oregon and Virginia filed separate lawsuits against SpaceX, alleging the company violated state consumer protection laws regarding Grok AI features on X. The complaints accuse SpaceX of ignoring industry-standard safeguards and falsely advertising platform safety.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1040255/x-grok-ai-deepfakes-oregon-virginia-lawsuit)

### DeepMind Uses Agent Teams to Accelerate Quantum Algorithms
Google DeepMind announced research utilizing agent teams and formal verification to improve quantum algorithms. The methods are designed to discover faster algorithms for applications in chemistry and fusion research.
Source: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/)

### Meta Hosts Open Source AI For Science Summit
