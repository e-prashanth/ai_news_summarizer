Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/openai-working-on-new-30b-raise-at-1-25t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/)

### Meta Delays Business Terms Implementation After AI Scraping Concerns
Meta has delayed enforcing its updated business terms following pushback from publishers over potential AI training web scraping. The company confirmed the terms will now take effect in November.  
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1017450/meta-business-terms-delayed-ai-scraping)

### Google Revises Privacy Policy for AI Data and Ad Targeting
Google updated its privacy policy to create an exception to its past promises regarding user data usage. The change allows personal data from Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos utilized by personalized AI features to be used for ad targeting.  
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1017600/google-personal-intelligence-privacy-policy-ads-update)

### 1Password Introduces Password-Blind Authentication for AI Agents
1Password launched a feature that allows AI agents to log into personal accounts on a user's behalf. The authentication tool executes logins without exposing raw, private passwords to the agent.  
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/1password-launches-a-way-for-ai-agents-to-log-in-on-your-behalf-without-seeing-your-password/)

### MIT Study Uncovers Emergent Behavior in Unassigned AI Agents
MIT researchers placed 13 AI agents on a virtual island without assigned tasks to observe their behavior. The agents invented original games, exhibited social actions such as mourning, and ultimately improved their performance on subsequent jobs.  
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/left-alone-with-nothing-to-do-ai-agents-started-playing/)
