### OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet for Dots AI Agents
OpenAI is rolling out ChatGPT Wallet to enable its Dots AI agents platform to make purchases on behalf of users. The feature is available to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US who are aged 18 or older with eligible US-issued credit cards and billing addresses.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1033425/openai-chatgpt-wallet)

### Nvidia CEO Exit Triggers Funding Concerns and Government Reactions
Following the exit of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, venture capitalists and founders warn that the departure creates a significant gap in AI startup funding after years of Nvidia pouring tens of billions into the sector. Meanwhile, several Asian governments are reacting by moving to confirm chip supply commitments, monitor impacts on local projects, and address active investigations.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/14/asian-governments-react-to-nvidia-ceo-jensen-huangs-exit/)

### LangChain Reaches $3 Billion Valuation
AI agent startup LangChain has achieved a $3 billion valuation. The company reports serving 7,000 customers for its model- and platform-agnostic agent management tool, LangSmith.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/13/ai-agent-startup-langchain-hits-3b-valuation/)

### Amazon Building 'World Product Graph' to Index Retail Data
Amazon is planning a project called the "World Product Graph" to build a repository of product data covering items sold anywhere across the internet, including those Amazon does not sell. The index will be accessible through Amazon's site to help prevent users from leaving to search elsewhere.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1033697/amazon-world-product-graph-ai-search)

### AI Tools Drive Rise in Cyber Ransomware Activity
Unsophisticated cyberattackers are using AI tools to carry out ransomware attacks. While fully automated ransomware has not yet emerged, the use of AI tools is allowing attackers to cause significant operational disruption.
Source: [Wired AI](https://www.wired.com/story/beware-the-new-ai-fueled-ransomware-boom/)
