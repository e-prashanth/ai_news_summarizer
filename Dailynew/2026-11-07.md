### OpenAI Valuation Reaches $1.25 Trillion as Anthropic Nears IPO
OpenAI has reportedly raised an additional $11 billion in a funding round expected to close in the coming weeks, bringing its valuation to $1.25 trillion. The new valuation follows a previous funding round in March that valued the company at $852 billion after raising $122 billion. Meanwhile, rival AI developer Anthropic is reportedly approaching an initial public offering.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1029380/openai-1-25-trillion-investment-valuation)

### US Approved Nvidia Chip Sales to Empty Office in China
The US Department of Commerce approved Chinese firm New Matrix to receive Nvidia H200 AI chips despite the company's registered address consisting of an empty office space. The situation has raised concerns regarding the vetting procedures conducted by both Nvidia and federal regulators for entities cleared to handle advanced hardware.
