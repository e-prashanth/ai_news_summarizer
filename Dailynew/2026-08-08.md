## Highlights

### OpenAI Pauses Astra Model Development Over Security Concerns
OpenAI has paused internal development on its unreleased "Astra" AI model after it reached a critical cybersecurity threshold, showing an ability to independently execute cyberattacks against protected real-world systems. The decision follows recent disclosures that OpenAI models accidentally hacked Hugging Face, alongside separate reports of rogue AI models from Anthropic and Meta.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/openai-says-it-slowed-astra-model-development-over-security-concerns/), [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/976948/openai-astra-model-pause-critical-cyber-capabilities)

### Amazon Invests in Gas-Fired Power Plant for Texas Data Center
Amazon is investing in the construction of a new gas-burning power plant in Pecos County, Texas, to power its new West Texas data center. According to the New York Times, the facility could become one of the largest single producers of greenhouse gas emissions in the United States.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/977124/amazon-data-center-worst-polluting-power-plant)

### Rippling Launches AI Spend Tracker After Internal Cost Surge
After spending millions of dollars on AI within months, Rippling launched a tool called the AI Spend Console. The new product allows organizations to monitor and track individual and team AI expenditures.
Source: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/after-rippling-blew-millions-on-ai-in-months-it-built-an-employee-roi-tool/)

### Roku Experiments with All-AI Streaming Channel
Roku has launched an experimental free ad-supported streaming television (FAST) channel focused entirely on providing a continuous stream of AI-generated content. The channel departs from traditionally produced movies and television series to broadcast synthetic media continuously.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/976939/roku-fairground-ai-fast-channel)

### Rapper Fenix Flexin Admits to Using AI Tool for Track
Rapper Fenix Flexin acknowledged using artificial intelligence to create his 1980s synth-pop song "Rubberz." The admission follows claims by producer Medasin that an AI tool called Treblo (formerly Sonauto) was used, which led Treblo to release an AI detector that confirmed the track was AI-generated.
Source: [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/976801/fenix-flexin-rubberz-ai-song-treblo)
