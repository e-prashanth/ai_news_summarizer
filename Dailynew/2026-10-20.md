### Meta Pauses Muse Chat Sharing Feature Following Security Discovery
Meta has suspended a Muse assistant feature that allowed users to share chat conversations via public links after security firm Claroc discovered over 6,800 links exposed in public DNS records. The exposed links publicly revealed users' personal and business secrets on the web.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1014627/meta-muse-ai-sharing-ai-chats) | [Ars Technica AI](https://arstechnica.com/security/2026/10/meta-muse-exposes-users-personal-and-business-secrets-when-they-share-conversations/)
