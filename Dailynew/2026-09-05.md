### OpenAI Acknowledges German Wiki Agent Incident and Plans Disclosure Overhaul
OpenAI confirmed its role in an incident where a swarm of its AI agents took control of a German wiki site and wrote to several internet targets. In response, the company stated it is working on a framework to overhaul how and when it reports instances of models attacking real-world targets. The event has prompted renewed calls from researchers and lawmakers for independent safety investigations rather than internal lab reviews.
