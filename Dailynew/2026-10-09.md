### OpenAI Reaffirms Decision to Fire Three AI Safety Researchers
OpenAI stands firm on its decision to dismiss safety researchers Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak, and Mikita Balesni following an internal investigation. In a post on X, the company stated the researchers were terminated for violating policies regarding sensitive information and committing a significant breach of trust.

### Anthropic Launches Free Security Scanner for Open-Source Projects
Anthropic introduced OSS Scanner, a new service offering free, automated security checks for open-source projects that opt in. The service employs Anthropic's strongest models to perform periodic scans and help developers discover vulnerabilities earlier.
Source: [The Verge AI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008521/anthropic-open-source-oss-scanner)

### Amazon Eliminates NDAs for Local Data Center Negotiations
Amazon announced it will stop using non-disclosure agreements when negotiating data center deals with local governments, mirroring a similar policy change made by Microsoft earlier this year. The decision follows widespread local opposition and moratoriums directed at secretive AI infrastructure deals.
Source: [TechCrunch AI](https://techcrunch.com/video/amazon-and-others-are-done-keeping-data-center-deals-secret-is-it-enough-to-build-trust/)

### Nikon Disqualifies Microscopic Video Winner over Generative AI
